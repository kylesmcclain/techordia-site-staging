Frequently asked questions

Managed IT questions we hear often

What happens when we switch providers?

We start with discovery, access cleanup, documentation, backup review, monitoring setup, and a practical transition plan. The goal is to make the change feel controlled instead of disruptive.

Do users get real human support?

Yes. Support is handled by people who learn your business technology, explain what is happening, and stay with the issue until it is resolved.

Is cybersecurity included in managed IT?

Security is built into the operating rhythm. We maintain core controls around sign-ins, devices, email, backups, patching, training, and response planning.
